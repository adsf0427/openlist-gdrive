Privacy Policy

OpenList Drive Connector is a personal OAuth client used only by its owner to connect a self-hosted OpenList server to the owner's own Google Drive.

  • It accesses Google Drive files only to list, upload and download them through the owner's OpenList server.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on that server. No data is sent to any third party.
  • No data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
